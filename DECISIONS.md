# Decisions

Newest at the bottom. One entry per decision: what, why, what it replaced.

## 2026-11-11 : Founding session (in VIVEIRO)

**D1. The "host a builders night" plan is abandoned.**
Cass's first answer said Fortaleza had no AI-builders night and told Manos to start one. Cass's own search results listed a GDG Fortaleza monthly AI and Machine Learning meetup, so the premise was probably false. The plan also solved a different problem (community building) than the one asked (friends matched both ways).

**D2. Friendship is treated as a property of a history, not of a person.**
Nobody is a friend-in-waiting. The system can find candidates and it can protect the hours. It cannot find friends.

**D3. "Friend" is defined by three observable behaviours.**
Hours together, their invitations, and recent contact. A nine-trait scoring table was written and then retired: with no rule for combining the rows it could not be proven wrong.

**D4. Stake v1 withdrawn.**
v1 claimed 3 of 12 gated candidates would become friends in 180 days. Review found two fatal problems. Its 225-hour budget funded only 4 people to the 50-hour mark, so passing needed 3 of 4 to succeed: an 11% chance at the claimed one-in-three rate, not the 81% stated. And a weekly group activity could produce seven "friends" from one table.

**D5. Stake v2 claims the outcome of the whole procedure.**
Not the gate. A single run with no control cannot separate the gate from the effort, so v2 does not try.

**D6. Manos's historical rate is context only.**
With a handful of past cases, a "double the baseline" clause swings the pass bar from trivial to nearly impossible.

**D7. No names or personal details of other people in git.**
The first note about this was pushed to the eStudio37 org repo without being asked. The rule exists so it does not happen again with other people's data.

**D8. Named NETLEASURE and graduated from VIVEIRO.**
Manos's call, same day. Lineage stub: `VIVEIRO/graduated/seed-20261111-netleasure-friends-fortaleza.md`.
