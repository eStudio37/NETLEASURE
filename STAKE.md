## The claim

**By August 31, 2027, Manos has at least 2 new friends in Fortaleza, for no more than 250 leisure hours.**

It is a claim about the whole procedure (sourcing, gate, time invested). It claims nothing about the gate on its own.

## "Friend", measured on August 31, 2027

All three must hold for a person to count:

1. **Hours:** 50 or more leisure hours together, counted only one-to-one or in groups of four or fewer. Work time does not count.
2. **Reciprocity:** they initiated at least 4 contacts in the last 90 days, outside any standing event.
3. **Alive:** you met in the last 30 days.

"New" means the first meeting happened on or after 2026-11-11.

## Outcomes and what each one decides

| Result | Condition | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Pass | 2 or more friends, 250 hours or fewer | Keep the procedure and write it up as the method. |
| Fail on count | Fewer than 2, with 150 to 250 hours spent | Drop the procedure. The log shows which stage leaked. |
| Fail on cost | More than 250 hours | It works too expensively. Drop it. |
| Void | Fewer than 150 hours spent | Untested. The constraint is time, not matching. |

Hours counted toward the 250 and the 150 are all leisure hours logged with any candidate, including first meetings that went nowhere.

## The plan behind the number (assumptions, not stakes)

| Stage | People | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| First meetings, 2 hours each | 20 | 40 |
| Trials, 8 more hours each | 8 | 64 |
| Commitments, 40 more hours each | 3 | 120 |
| Total | | 224 |

About 6 hours a week for 42 weeks. Two of the three commitments must succeed. If each has a 75% chance, the claim passes 84% of the time. At 50% each, it passes half the time. There is no evidence for either figure.

## Known weaknesses

- **Sourcing does not exist yet.** It is the part most likely to kill the claim, and it depends on contacts only Manos can list.
- **The ground truth is one-sided.** The instrument check rests on Manos's judgment of who is a friend. Only the invitation count reflects the other person.
- **Single run, no control.** A pass shows the goal was met. It does not show which part of the procedure did it.
- **The other people do not know.** The log must hold only Manos's hours and their invitation dates. Nobody is dropped because of the test.
